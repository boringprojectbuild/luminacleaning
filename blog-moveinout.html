What Landlords Actually Look For During Move Out Inspections

By Lumina Cleaning Service · June 2026 · 5 min read

After years of cleaning homes and apartments across San Antonio, we've developed a pretty clear picture of what property managers focus on during move out inspections. The good news: most of it is completely preventable with a thorough professional clean. The bad news: most tenants don't know what they're missing until the deposit check comes up short.

The Areas Landlords Always Check First

Property managers in San Antonio have seen it all. They know where tenants cut corners — and they go straight for those spots. Here's what gets flagged most often during move out inspections:

  • Kitchen appliances inside and out
  • Grease and buildup on stovetops and hoods
  • Bathroom grout, tile, and caulk
  • Baseboards throughout the unit
  • Inside cabinets and drawers
  • Ceiling fans and light fixtures
  • Window sills and tracks
  • Walls — scuffs, marks, and residue

Why DIY Cleaning Often Isn't Enough

Most tenants clean their apartments before moving out. The problem isn't effort — it's knowing what to look for. Professional move out cleaning in San Antonio means a systematic, top-to-bottom approach that covers every surface landlords actually inspect. We've helped dozens of San Antonio tenants walk away with their full deposit.

  • All floors swept, mopped, vacuumed
  • Baseboards and ceiling fans dusted
  • Inside cabinets and drawers cleaned
  • Windows and window sills wiped
  • Walls spot cleaned
